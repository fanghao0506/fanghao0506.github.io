Welcome

Hao Fang

PhD Candidate @ University of Edinburgh

I am a PhD candidate in the School of Engineering at the University of Edinburgh, advised by Dr. Yunjie Yang, working on machine learning methods for next-generation pulmonary electrical impedance tomography (EIT). My research sits at the intersection of medical imaging, inverse problems, digital twins, and deep learning, with a particular focus on high-quality 3D and 4D lung function reconstruction from low-dimensional voltage measurements.

Before starting my PhD, I worked as a Software and Algorithm Engineer at the Brain-Machine Fusion Intelligence Institute, Jiangsu Industrial Technology Research Institute, where I developed software and algorithms for brain-computer interface systems. I received my MSc in Signal Processing and Communications from the University of Edinburgh, advised by Dr. Elliot J. Crowley, and my BEng in Electrical and Electronic Engineering from Southwest Jiaotong University, advised by Prof. Deqing Huang.

  • Intelligent Sensing and Physical AI: learning-enabled perception, EIT-based sensing, electronic skin, and soft robotic tactile/proprioceptive intelligence.
  • Digital Twins and Complex System Modeling: CT-derived anatomical modeling, COMSOL/FEM simulation, respiratory dynamics, and Sim2Clinic data-generation pipelines.
  • Generative AI and Agent Systems: LLM/Agent evaluation, shopping-agent benchmarks, grounding, trajectory quality, and applied AI product workflows.
  • Computer Vision and Medical Imaging: pulmonary EIT, 3D/4D reconstruction, clinical bedside monitoring, image analysis, and inverse imaging problems.

Projects

Precision 4D Pulmonary Functional Imaging

Medical imaging + machine learning + digital twins, 2023-present

Builds a Sim2Clinic data-generation pipeline for 4D pulmonary EIT: segmenting lungs, thoracic cavity, and multi-tissue structures from large-scale clinical CT; constructing individualized thorax-electrode FEM models in COMSOL; and generating large-scale 4D EIT datasets through physiology-informed respiratory dynamics simulations.

Develops modular training paradigms, multimodal single-frame reconstruction models, and temporal autoregressive deep reconstruction models to recover high-quality 3D/4D lung function images from low-dimensional voltage sequences.

Models realistic measurement disturbances and introduces patient-adaptive strategies, validating robustness and generalization on multi-center clinical datasets.

Clinical Bedside Real-time Medical Imaging

Industry-university collaboration, 2024-present

Develops 3D lung EIT reconstruction algorithms for real-time ICU bedside imaging with Prunus Medical and the University of Edinburgh.

SELECT: Next-generation Soft Robots
