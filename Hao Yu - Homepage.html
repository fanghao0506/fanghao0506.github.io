    🥰 Welcome!

    Hao Yu

    Research Associate @ University of Edinburgh

    I am a Postdoctoral Research Associate and Edinburgh Generative AI Laboratory Fellow, in the School of Engineering in the Institute for Imaging, Data and Communications (IDCOM) at the University of Edinburgh, supervised by Dr. Yunjie Yang. Previously, I completed my PhD at University of Edinburgh, advised by Dr. Jiabin Jia (co-advised by Dr. Chang Liu). Prior to that, I obtained my Master's degree from Harbin Institute of Technology, supervised by Prof. Jibin Zou and co-supervised by Prof. Guodong Yu.

    My research focuses on physical AI driven by instrumentation and measurement, exploring how AI can be empowered by instrument-based observations to perceive and understand complex, multiscale physical systems—from individual health to industrial processes and environmental sensing.

    • Physical AI for Sensing: Industrial Processes, Equipment Health, Healthcare, and Environmental Remote Sensing
    • Trustworthy AI: Reliable and Interpretable Deep Learning and Large Language Models

    📢 I am seeking highly self-motivated PhD, Master and Bachelor students or collaborators interested in intelligent sensing, trustworthy AI, and LLMs. Feel free to reach out via email if you are interested in potential research collaborations.
